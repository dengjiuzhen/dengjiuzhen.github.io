Serious Game / Gameful Self-Regulation / App Concept / 2025

Dream Manor: Echoing Fields

Gameful & Participatory Design MDA Framework Creative Construction & Social Play

A serious game app concept that turns personal goals into a 2D construction world, making everyday effort visible through play while allowing players to set their own pace.

Problem

Overloaded to-do lists can make progress feel abstract and difficult to sustain.

Design Objective

Translate personal goals into playable tasks and visible changes in a world players build themselves.

Design Lens

The Mechanics–Dynamics–Aesthetics (MDA) framework connects goal structure, player behavior, and the felt experience of progress.

From Goals to Playable Systems

Game–Life Mapping

Real-life goals become tasks with timelines that make effort and pacing visible.

Resource Systems

Task completion earns Mana Sparks; collected materials support construction.

Construction Rules

Placement rules shape the world, while building durability reflects goal completion.

Player Control

Goals and difficulty can be adjusted without penalties when circumstances change.

Plot Mode

Experiment with construction through free play.

Development Mode

Use collectible objects to visualize achieved goals.

Social Mode

Visit friends’ spaces and connect progress with peer encouragement and optional rankings.

Player Experience — Design Hypotheses

The design explores how feedback, pacing, and player choice might support meaningful engagement rather than short-lived rewards.

Engagement & Flow

  • Micro-tasks make daily action more approachable.
  • Feedback gives repeated effort a visible rhythm.
  • Adjustable challenge aims to sustain focus.

Self-Regulation & Agency

  • Players recalibrate plans to fit changing needs.
  • Autonomy balances accountability.
  • Creative choices make progress personally meaningful.

Affective & Narrative Design

Meaningful play = visible transformation + earned progress

Emotional

Sensory

Soft colors and music set the pace for an inviting experience.

Symbolic

Narrative

Design Rationale & Game Design Document

Psychological Intention

Externalize goals to make effort easier to recognize and reflect upon.

Behavioral Design

Connect manageable tasks with feedback loops that encourage follow-through.

MDA Alignment

Link game mechanics to choices and emotions, rather than treating rewards as the sole source of motivation.

Creative & Social Meaning

Turn achievements into spatial memories that players can personalize and optionally share.
