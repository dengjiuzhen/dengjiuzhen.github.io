Dyed in Rain: Collaborations in Ink and Code

Logline

An experimental animation created in collaboration with traditional ink painting tools and digital scanning systems, where water, ink, and computation co-author the image.

As the work dissolves and transforms, it evokes memory, rain, and the quiet inevitability of being dyed into the currents of social life.

Medium

Ink, water, paper, scanner, code

Concept

  • Material and digital systems co-produce imagery
  • Transformation replaces control
  • Process becomes authorship
