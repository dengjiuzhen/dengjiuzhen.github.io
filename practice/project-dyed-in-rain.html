Experimental Animation / Collaborations in Ink and Code

Dyed in Rain

Ink & Computational Processes Experimental Animation Process-Based Authorship

An experimental animation created through ink, water, digital scanning, and code, exploring how material behavior and computational processes can co-author moving images.

Experimental animation · Watch on YouTube

Artistic Approach

Working across traditional ink painting, scanning, and code, I explored how material unpredictability and computational transformation can shape an image beyond my initial intention.

Concept

As the work dissolves and transforms, it evokes memory, rain, and the quiet inevitability of being dyed into the currents of social life.

Medium

Ink, water, paper, scanner, code
